# The Art of War — Sun Tzu (Giles translation, 1910)

> The oldest known treatise on strategy: how to win by planning, positioning,
> deception, and intelligence rather than by force alone.

## Bibliographic

- **Author(s):** Sun Tzu (tr. Lionel Giles)
- **Year:** c. 5th century BC (this translation 1910)
- **Source file:** [[../../raw/books/sun-tzu-art-of-war/book.md]]

## Author's thesis

War is a vital matter of state to be studied, not left to chance. Victory is
decided before the battle by calculation, positioning, and knowledge of both
sides. The supreme skill is to subdue the enemy with the least fighting —
through deception, terrain, and foreknowledge.

## Key concepts introduced or developed

- [[Deception-in-Strategy]] — the whole art rests on it
- [[Foreknowledge]] — knowing the enemy in advance, via spies
- [[Terrain]] — classifying ground and adapting to it
